Week 6 · Interactivity

A histogram shows the distribution of annual TV energy use. Select a display technology to update the histogram and scatterplot; hover or focus on a scatter point to inspect a model.

6.1–6.2 · Energy use histogram and filters

Each 100 kWh bin counts TV models. The y-axis stays fixed so filtered groups are directly comparable. Values above 1,800 kWh/year are omitted from this histogram and reported below.

6.3–6.4 · Star rating versus energy use

Point colour indicates display technology. Point tooltip shows brand, model, size, star rating and annual energy use. All valid values, including high energy outliers, appear here.
