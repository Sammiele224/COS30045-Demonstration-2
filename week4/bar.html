Exercises 4.3–4.6 · Scaled bar chart

D3 reads a CSV of model counts, binds one row to each rectangle, and uses a linear scale for width and a band scale for vertical placement.

Most common TV brands

Number of models in the course TV dataset. Brand spellings are normalised to uppercase.

Source: course Ex6_TVdata_withStar.csv, aggregated to brand_counts.csv.

What to explain in the demonstration

d3.csv() loads rows as strings; +d.count converts counts to numbers. scaleLinear() maps a count to a rectangle width. scaleBand() maps each brand to a row and supplies the bar height. The SVG viewBox scales the result with the page.
