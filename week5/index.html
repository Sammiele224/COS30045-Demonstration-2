Week 5 · Four chart types

The charts use axes, labels, scales, SVG circles and paths, and a pie layout for donut slices. Hover or focus a mark for details; click it or press Enter or Space to keep it selected.

5.1 · Vertical bar chart

Average annual energy consumption for 55 inch TVs, grouped by display technology.

Select a bar to inspect its average and model count.

5.2 · Scatter plot

Annual Australian wholesale spot price observations, 1998–2024.

Select a point to inspect its year and price.

5.2 · Line chart

The same observations connected in chronological order.

Select a point on the line to inspect its year and price.

5.3 · Donut chart

Share of TV models by small, medium and large screen size.

Select a slice to inspect its share of TV models.

Course sources: Ex6_TVdata_withStar.csv for TV charts and ARE_Spot_Prices.csv for the 1998–2024 price charts. The price series uses the supplied average column, excluding Tasmania and Snowy.
